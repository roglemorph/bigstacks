Corporate Bond Listings

Five issuers trade at once. Each $1,000 bill issue lists ~10,000 bills and stays open until sold out (or the issuer defaults); then a new issue replaces it. Listed yields refresh each quarter; your held coupon stays fixed at purchase. Issuer bankruptcy wipes unsold bills and resolves your holdings at a recovery rate by rating.
