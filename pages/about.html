This is a game about investing, I guess. It is inspired by the suprising good educational game called 'Build your STAX' here: https://buildyourstax.com/. The game is not in a finsihed state and I am not sure if it will ever be. That being said, I would like to be somewhat educational in the future. Comments and suggestions are appreciated greatly. This project is entirely 'vibe coded', that is to say I did not write any of the code by hand (although I have put a good bit of thought in the design). It is 'open source' but I do not reccomend you read the code, it is likely a mess. Some tips on gameplay: Do not let cash sit idly Bonds are crucial for generating income. You recieve $1000 per year in income, and the only way the only way to scale that income is to get bonds. You should have autobuy on for the index fund and bonds always.
